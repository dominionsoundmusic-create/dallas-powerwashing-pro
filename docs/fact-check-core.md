## Not changed, for Maurice to decide

- Pesticide licensing. The pages say Texas has no state license for pressure washing, which matches the Governor's office and TDLR. I could not find a clear official answer on whether a company applying algae or mildew killers in a soft wash needs a Texas Department of Agriculture or structural pest control license. The pages make no claim either way. If you want the license guide to cover it, it needs a TDA source first.
- HomeAdvisor's main page (www.homeadvisor.com/cost/painting/powerwash-exterior-surfaces/) timed out on a permission request. I checked the same article through HomeAdvisor's own cost-guide-ssr mirror. The link in site.json was not changed.
- The hail source is a 2012 news story. The pages now say 2012. Swap it if a newer Insurance Council of Texas figure turns up.
