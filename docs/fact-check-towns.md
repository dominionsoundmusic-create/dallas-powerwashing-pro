## Not changed (outside my files)
- The Frisco page says the city's stormwater hotline handles pollution reports. That is true (City of Frisco stormwater FAQ, 972-292-5800, https://www.friscotexas.gov/Faq.aspx?TID=32), but there is no source key for it in `src/data/site.json`, which I was not allowed to edit. To credit it, add a key such as `frisco_sw` to site.json and add it to the Frisco page's `sources` list.
- The `hail` source label in site.json says "Insurance Council of Texas data, 2012". The article credits the 800 count to National Weather Service records, along with the Insurance Council. The label could say "(NWS and Insurance Council of Texas data, 2012)".
