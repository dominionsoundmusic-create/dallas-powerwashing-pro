## Wash water rules
- Dallas code, stormwater article (amlegal 0-0-0-109726; section number not shown in the text returned): offense to discharge water that is not entirely stormwater. Defense (b)(14): water used in washing streets, sidewalks, parking lots, driveways or other structures not contaminated with soap, detergent, degreaser, solvent, emulsifier, dispersant or a harmful quantity of other cleaning substance. Prohibited (f)(6)(E): mobile power washing if wastewater contains harmful quantity of those; (f)(6)(G) pavement washdown likewise. Burden of proof is on the discharger. https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/dallas/latest/dallas_tx/0-0-0-109726
- Dallas stormwater flyer: wash water can go to storm drain or street only if no cleaning chemicals, clean enough not to harm the creek, no erosion or muddy runoff. Plan the water path; barriers (weighted boards, sandbags); chemicals: block drains and collect all water (wet vacuum); water only: block drains, divert to grass. 214-948-4022, stormwater@dallascityhall.com https://dallascityhall.com/departments/waterutilities/stormwater-operations/PublishingImages/Keep%20Stormwater%20Clean%20Feb%2023_2021.pdf
- Fort Worth: mobile commercial cosmetic cleaners need a permit: $25 business + $25 per wash unit, valid 1 year, not transferable; proof of insurance for vehicles, driver's licenses; registration numbers at least 3 inches tall on each side of each registered vehicle. Wash water must not leave the property or enter storm drain except narrow exemption: cold water no soap etc., screened (20 mesh), through oil boom. Hot over 110 F needs prior permission. Plain-water washing of parking lots/gas stations will likely still violate. Permit violations up to $2,000 per occurrence; illicit discharge up to $2,000 per day per offense. Sanitary sewer needs owner permission, 400 micron filter, never manholes. Biodegradable detergents not allowed to storm drain. 1993 study: detergents at over 50% of 560 sites. 817-392-6309, CosmeticCleaner@fortworthtexas.gov, pollution hotline 817-392-1234. https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/environmental-quality/stormwater-quality/powerwash
- Fort Worth best practices PDF: absorb oil with clay first; collect water with boom + sump or wet vac. https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/files/assets/public/code-compliance/env/documents/code-environmental-commercial-cosmetic-cleaner-best-practices-2021.pdf
- Frisco Sec 34-331(a) only stormwater; 34-331(b)(14) street wash water is an affirmative defense; 34-332(d)(5) mobile power washer wastewater with soap or other harmful cleaners; 34-332(d) wastewater from pavement with unremoved spill; 34-332(g) service station pavement wash water through interceptor; 34-339(a) civil up to $1,000/day, up to $5,000/day for discharges; 34-339(b) criminal up to $2,000 per violation per day. https://friscotexas.gov/DocumentCenter/View/22094/ARTICLE_IX__STORMWATER_DISCHARGEdoc
- Frisco HOAs maintain many ponds, creeks and stormwater features; low oxygen can cause fish kills; Stormwater Division 972-292-5800. https://www.friscotexas.gov/1870/HOA-Stormwater-Management
- Addison Chapter 72 (Ord. 010016, June 8 2010): pollutants banned from storm drains; exempt list covers individual residential vehicle washing, says nothing on pavement washing or mobile services; fine up to $500, each day separate. https://gis.addisontx.gov/GIS_Documents/Ordinances/2010/010-016%20Amend%20Chapter%2072,%20Illicit%20Discharges%20to%20Strmwtr%20Drainage%20System.pdf
- Arlington: "Never hose off the driveway or street with water to clean a spill or leak"; storm drains and sanitary sewers separate; report 817-459-6777. https://www.arlingtontx.gov/Government/Departments/Department-Directory/Stormwater-Management/Stormwater-Education/Community-Healthy-Watersheds-Program/Stormwater-Pollution-Prevention
- Denton: drains lead to Lewisville Lake, a primary drinking water source for much of DFW; car wash wastewater listed as common illicit discharge; report Denton 311. https://www.cityofdenton.com/248/Residential-Watershed-Protection
- Denton publishes a manual titled "BMPs for Pressure Washing and Surface Cleaning" (title seen in search, NOT OPENED) https://www.cityofdenton.com/DocumentCenter/View/388/Pressure-Wash-Manual-PDF
- McKinney: Wilson Creek and East Fork Trinity River listed as impaired by bacteria; about 53,000 dogs estimated; runoff can start with about 0.1 inch of rain; yard waste clogs drains. https://www.mckinneytexas.org/305/Pollution-Sources
- Keller: never pour or sweep anything into a storm drain; report 817-743-4092 or -4094, after 4:30 817-743-4522. https://www.cityofkeller.com/services/public-works/environmental-services/stormwater-quality
- Grapevine: Lake about 3.6 sq mi adjoining, 25 miles shoreline in city, northern drainage flows to the lake, 65.3 miles of creeks; report 817-410-3330, after hours 817-410-8127. https://www.grapevinetexas.gov/DocumentCenter/View/11598/2025-08-28-FINAL-Grapevine-2025-MS4-SWMP
- Southlake: stormwater is not treated; drains separate from sewers; HOAs maintain private ponds; Public Works (817) 748-8098. https://cityofsouthlake.com/3833/Drainage-FAQs . ## Water supply and restrictions

Fines $250 to $2,000, first offense warning. Report 3-1-1. https://savedallaswater.com/resource-center/ordinance/ , https://dallascityhall.com/departments/waterutilities/Water%20Bill%20Insert/25_26%20INSERTS/06%20-%20MAR/Final_Time%20of%20Day%20Restrictions_Insert_2026_DIGITAL.pdf

- NTMWD 2024 plan: same Stage 3 wording; no restriction at stages 1, 2. Members: Allen, Farmersville, Forney, Frisco, Garland, McKinney, Mesquite, Plano, Princeton, Richardson, Rockwall, Royse City, Wylie. Ord 19-19 Stage 2 "Prohibit hosing off of paved areas, buildings, windows, and other surfaces." https://www.flowermound.gov/DocumentCenter/View/158/Town-of-Flower-Mound-Ordinance-19-19-Water-Conservation

Eastern Cross Timbers pass through eastern Denton and Tarrant counties and western Dallas county. https://www.tshaonline.org/handbook/entries/cross-timbers

## Surfaces
- ARMA: algae mainly Gloeocapsa magma, brown to black; do not use power washers; "High-pressure washing systems are likely to damage asphalt roofing"; dilute bleach lightens, temporary; friction loosens granules; recommends trained pros. https://www.asphaltroofing.org/algae-discoloration/
- Bob Vila: starts on north slopes and under trees; cover plants. https://www.bobvila.com/articles/black-roof-stains-check-for-algae/
- Fixr: soft washing typically under 500 PSI, wider tip, $0.25 to $0.35 per sq ft. https://www.fixr.com/costs/pressure-wash-house
- James Hardie: under 1500 psi, wide fan tip, at least 6 feet from wall. https://www.jameshardie.ca/JamesHardieMainSite/media/Site-Documents/TechnicalBulletins/20-james-hardie-siding-cleaning-and-maintenance.pdf
- Vinyl Siding Institute 2015: aim straight at eye level; angled spray can push water behind; some makers prohibit pressure washers. https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/when-thoughts-turn-to-spring-cleaning-6-simple-tips-can-keep-your-vinyl-siding-looking-new-300062053.html
- Standards for historic: Standard 7 gentlest means; sandblasting and high-pressure water not recommended on masonry; low-pressure water with mild detergent. https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/files/assets/public/v/2/development-services/documents/all-preservation-and-design/historic/secretary-interior-standards-treatment-historic-homes.pdf
- HGTV deck: softwood 500 to 600 PSI, harder up to 1,200 to 1,500; fan tip; 12 to 18 in; sand raised fibers. https://www.hgtv.com/remodel/outdoors/pressure-washing-a-deck
- Treated Wood (Viance): no more than 800 psi, 8 inches, fan 30 degrees or wider; experts differ. https://www.treatedwood.com/news/is-pressure-washing-bad-for-my-deck
- Trex: high-performance lines max 3100 psi fan tip 8 in; early-generation not recommended, warranty void. https://www.trex.com/care
- Fort Worth district count 14 incl. Fairmount, Stockyards, Mistletoe Heights. https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/development-services/historic-preservation . Fairmount 375 acres, built 1885 to 1940, wood-frame bungalows. https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/files/assets/public/v/1/development-services/documents/all-preservation-and-design/historic/historic-districts/fairmount/section1-introduction.pdf
- McKinney: COA for visible changes in Historic Overlay; ordinary maintenance not restricted; check with office 972-547-2000; painting unpainted masonry discouraged. https://www.mckinneytexas.org/m/faq?cat=50
