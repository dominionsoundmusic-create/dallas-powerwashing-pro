{%- endmacro %} {% macro company_check() -%}

Check the company yourself. Texas does not list power washing among the trades licensed by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation, so there is no state license number to look up. Ask for a certificate of insurance instead, ask how the wash water will be kept out of storm drains, get the price and scope in writing, and look up the business name on the Texas Comptroller's account status search (new tab). Dallas Power Washing Pro does not run these checks for you.

{%- endmacro %} {% macro safety() -%}

Safety basics. Stay off ladders and roofs. A wet roof is slippery, and a pressure washer pushes back when the trigger is pulled. Keep water away from outlets, meters and overhead lines, and treat every overhead wire as live. If your home was built before 1978, its paint may contain lead, so talk to the company before anyone washes painted surfaces.
