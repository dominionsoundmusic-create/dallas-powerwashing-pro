Dallas Power Washing Pro ("we," "us," or "our") is a d/b/a of Dominion Digital Group and operates dallaspowerwashingpro.com and the phone line published on it. Dallas Power Washing Pro is a free phone line, not a pressure washing company: it answers calls and connects callers with independent local pressure washing companies. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and the choices you have. This page is provided for transparency and is not legal advice.

Information we collect

Information you give us on a call. When you call the number on this site, the automated assistant records what you tell it, such as your name, callback number, city, and the work you need, so the call can be routed and the company you are connected with can follow up.

Call records. We retain the phone number you call from and a record of the call, including the time and how it was routed.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, this site and its analytics provider log information such as IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed and referring site. It is used in aggregate to understand how the site is performing, including counting taps on the phone number.

This site does not use contact or quote forms.

How your information is used

  • To route your call to the right kind of company and pass your request to it
  • To let that company call you back if it cannot pick up
  • To improve this website and the phone service
  • To meet legal, tax and record-keeping obligations

Text messages

If you provide your mobile number and consent to text messages, you may receive messages about your request, such as a confirmation or a follow-up. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and reply HELP for assistance. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase. We do not sell or share mobile numbers or SMS consent with third parties for their own marketing.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. When you call, we share your name, phone number, location and the details of the job with the independent local company we connect you with, so that it can contact you and quote the work. Otherwise we share information only with service providers who help us operate, such as phone, website hosting and analytics providers, and only to the extent they need it to perform that work. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies to measure traffic, and a language cookie if you choose the Spanish (ES) option, which uses Google Translate. Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Turning off cookies may affect how parts of this site work.

Data retention and security

We keep call records for as long as needed to handle your request and to meet legal and accounting requirements. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect the information we hold, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, subject to records we are required to keep. Texas residents may have additional rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act. To make a request, call {{ site.business.phone_display }}.

Children

This site is intended for adults arranging services for a property. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows the most recent version.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Call {{ site.business.phone_display }}. See also our terms.
