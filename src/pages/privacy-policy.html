1. The business behind this site

Dominion Digital Group owns Dallas Power Washing Pro and runs dallaspowerwashingpro.com and the number {{ site.business.phone_display }}. In this policy, "we" means that business, and "a company" means an independent power washing business a caller gets connected with. The line is a free referral service: no washing, no property visits, no pricing and no payments.

2. Phone calls

An automated assistant picks up this shared Dallas line and asks what the call is about. Calls may be recorded. A call record can include your name, the number you dial from, any other number you give, your city or area, the kind of work you want, and when you called. Please do not give a card number or other sensitive details on the line.

3. Sharing with the company

A referral can not happen without passing on your details, so the company receiving your call hears a short spoken summary, and if it does not answer, it gets your message. Once it has them, that company follows its own privacy practices. Vendors that provide phone service, call routing, hosting and analytics may also handle records while doing their jobs. Your personal information is not sold. It may be disclosed if the law requires it, or to protect legal rights.

4. The request form

Our contact form collects a name, phone number, city or ZIP code, the job type and an optional note. Netlify, the site's host, stores the entry, and it reaches the site owner by text and email. It is then used to send your request to a local company that will phone you.

5. Text messages

If you provide your mobile number and consent to text messages, you may receive messages about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to texts is not a condition of buying anything. Mobile numbers and text consent are not sold or shared with third parties for their marketing.

6. Website analytics

Google Analytics 4 shows which pages get read, where visitors came from and what devices they use. The tag waits for a first interaction, or a few seconds, so that pages load fast. Tapping a phone number is logged as an event. Analytics sets cookies such as _ga. The hosting servers keep routine logs, for example IP address, browser type and time. Browser settings let you block or clear cookies, and Google offers an opt-out add-on.

7. The language buttons

ES uses Google Translate. Pressing it stores a googtrans cookie and sends the text of the page to Google. Nothing loads from Google Translate until then. EN removes the cookie.

8. How long records are kept, and your choices

Records are kept as long as needed to run the line and meet legal duties. Call {{ site.business.phone_display }} to ask what is held about you, or to have it corrected or deleted. Separately ask the company that contacted you about its records. Texas residents may have additional rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act.

9. Children

This service is for adults arranging exterior work. Information from children under 13 is not knowingly collected.

10. Updates

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions go to the same number above. The terms of use are a separate page.
