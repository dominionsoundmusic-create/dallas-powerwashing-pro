Keller's water rules reach pressure washing sooner

If you are planning pressure washing in Keller, start with the city's water restrictions page, because Stage 1 here already restricts hosing paved areas and buildings.

Many North Texas plans wait until Stage 3 to ban that kind of washing. Keller's page lists hosing of paved areas and buildings among the Stage 1 Water Watch restrictions, and the city says stages are triggered by the Tarrant Regional Water District. So a drought called far from Keller can shape what you do on your own driveway. A quick call to 817-743-4080 settles it.

The city also keeps a year-round watering schedule, with no watering on Mondays. That is about lawns, not washing, but the same page bans runoff onto streets and patios. Think about that if your job drains toward the curb.

Wash toward the lawn

Keller gives homeowners simple advice on washing a car, and the same idea helps with any wash job.

The city's stormwater page says to wash on the lawn so water soaks into the soil, use a spray nozzle to save water, and never pour or sweep anything into a storm drain. Ask the company to steer water from the drive toward the grass, away from the curb, and to skip soap where plain water will do.

Where a cleaner is needed, the water should be collected, as the City of Dallas flyer explains. And keep cleaner away from any grass you want to keep.

Dry summers, clay and the foundation

Even at Keller's Stage 3, the city still allows foundations and trees to be watered up to two hours a day by handheld hose, soaker hose or drip irrigation. That tells you something about how much homeowners here worry about dry ground and moving soil. When a house has been through a dry stretch, look closely at brick walls and the edge of the slab before you wash, since small cracks and open joints can let water in where it is not wanted. Tell the company about anything you notice.

Vehicle rules can catch out a mobile job too. At Stage 1 the city limits private vehicle washing to a bucket or a hose with a turnoff valve, and at Stage 3 vehicle washing is prohibited except for health or safety reasons. So if you also want a truck or trailer cleaned, say so when you ask about the stage.
