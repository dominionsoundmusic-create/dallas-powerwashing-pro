Pressure washing across Irving's very different neighborhoods

Irving is a Dallas County city of 256,684 people, the twelfth largest in Texas at the 2020 census, and part of DFW Airport lies inside its limits. It is really three places in one. Las Colinas, developed from a ranch beginning in 1972, is a planned district of more than 12,000 acres with office towers, the Mandalay Canal, Lake Carolyn and the Mustangs of Las Colinas sculpture. Valley Ranch is a master-planned area that was home to the Dallas Cowboys' headquarters from 1985 to 2016. And East Irving has the city's older neighborhoods.

This line connects you with an independent, locally owned pressure washing company for any of them. It does not clean anything itself. You call, an assistant transfers you, and the company quotes, does the work and is paid by you.

Three kinds of Irving jobs

  • East Irving homes: older brick and siding, driveways and walks. The Brick Industry Association says high pressure is not appropriate for brick, so expect a soft wash.
  • Valley Ranch and newer streets: stone fronts, fences, pool decks and drives.
  • Las Colinas and apartments: entries, canal-side walks, breezeways, stairs and parking, usually on a schedule and outside business hours.

Only rain down the drain

In a 2021 notice, the City of Irving said "the only thing that belongs in the storm drain is rain," that yard waste and other debris clog pipes and cause flooding, and that a city ordinance prohibits introducing pollutants into the storm drain system. Irving is in Dallas County next door to Dallas, whose own guidance spells out that soapy or chemical wash water must be kept out of drains and collected. Ask any company how it handles runoff before it starts.
