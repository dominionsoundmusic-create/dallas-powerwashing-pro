What changes from one DFW town to the next

  • Permits: Fort Worth requires a city permit for mobile commercial power washers. Most other cities regulate the water rather than the washer.
  • Water source and drought stages: Plano, Frisco, McKinney and Allen are North Texas Municipal Water District cities; Addison buys from Dallas; Denton draws from Lewisville and Ray Roberts lakes. Each has its own staged plan.
  • Storm drains: the regional council of governments says no local government in North Central Texas treats stormwater, so runoff goes straight to creeks and lakes everywhere.
  • Historic districts: Fort Worth and McKinney both have districts with design rules for exterior changes.
