1. Who we are and what the line does

Dallas Power Washing Pro is a d/b/a of Dominion Digital Group. It runs this website and a free phone line for the Dallas-Fort Worth area. An automated assistant answers each call, finds out what needs cleaning, and transfers the caller to an independent, locally owned pressure washing company. Dallas Power Washing Pro is a referral line only. It owns no cleaning equipment, has no crews, and never visits, inspects, prices or cleans a property.

2. The company you hire is responsible for the job

Each company the line connects you with runs its own business. That company, not Dallas Power Washing Pro, decides whether to take the job, looks at the property, sets the price, books the date, carries out the cleaning and answers for the result. Any written estimate, deposit, payment schedule, cancellation rule or workmanship promise comes from that company and binds only you and it. Dallas Power Washing Pro does not sign, enforce or take part in that arrangement.

3. Cost of using the line

Calling the line and being transferred is free. Callers are never billed by Dallas Power Washing Pro. Companies that receive calls may pay Dallas Power Washing Pro a referral fee.

4. Checking a company before you hire it

The State of Texas does not license pressure washing. Before work starts, ask the company to show current liability insurance and, for jobs in Fort Worth, its city permit for mobile commercial cleaning. Dallas Power Washing Pro does not license, certify or vouch for any company, and you decide whether to hire the company you are connected with.

5. What we do with what you tell us

The details you give on a call, such as your name, callback number, city and the job, are passed to the company receiving your call so it can reach you. The Privacy Policy explains this in full.
