Dallas Power Washing Pro is a free phone line and this website. It is not a pressure washing company. It does not own equipment, employ a crew, inspect, quote or schedule jobs. Its whole job is to answer your call any hour and connect you with an independent local company that does.

What the assistant asks

Because the same number takes several kinds of home service calls for the Dallas area, the assistant starts with what you need. For pressure washing it asks:

  • What needs cleaning: house, driveway, roof, fence, deck, storefront
  • Roughly how big it is: stories, cars, feet of fence, square feet
  • Your city or area within Dallas-Fort Worth
  • Your name and a callback number, in case the company cannot pick up

It does not quote prices or book appointments. Those come from the company.

Before you hire anyone

Texas has no state license for pressure washing, so ask any company for proof of liability insurance, a written price for each surface, which surfaces it will soft wash, and how it will keep wash water out of the storm drain. The Texas rules guide explains why those questions matter, and the cost guide shows published price ranges.
