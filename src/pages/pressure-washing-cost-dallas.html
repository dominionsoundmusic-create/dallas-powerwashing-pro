What pushes a Dallas quote up or down

Height

A second story means more time and more care. Angi's range jumps from $150 at one story to $400 at two.

Surface

Low-pressure work on wood or siding takes longer than concrete. Soft washing with a cleaner adds product costs.

Stains

HomeGuide lists oil removal at $25 to $50 per stain, and $20 to $75 to treat mold or algae.

Water handling

If soapy water must be collected, the job needs booms or a vacuum. That is common on commercial sites and adds time.

Access

Tight side yards, steep lots and no nearby water spigot can all slow a crew down.

Timing

Some companies may be busier in certain seasons. Ask if another week would make a difference.

How to compare two quotes fairly

A low quote can leave things out, and a high one can include things you do not need. Ask each company the same questions, and get the answers in writing.

  • Which surfaces are included, and is it one side or both?
  • What pressure, tip and cleaning products are planned?
  • Are stain treatment and sealing included or extra?
  • Who handles the wash water?
  • Can you show a certificate of insurance?
  • What happens if something is damaged?

If someone knocks on your door after a storm with an offer, Texas gives you three business days to cancel most door-to-door sales over $25. Read the Attorney General's page for details. A word on doing it yourself

HomeGuide's driveway guide lists a day's machine rental at $40 to $100. That looks cheap next to a quote, but the risk is on you: damaged siding, a cracked window seal or a fall. On anything above ground level, the safety note below matters more than the savings.

Is the lowest quote the right one?

Not always. Fixr reports whole-house figures of $500 to $900, which sit well above Angi's Dallas average, and that gap shows how differently these guides define a job. One may count a basic rinse of a small house, and another a full wash of a larger one. So compare what is included, not only the total.
