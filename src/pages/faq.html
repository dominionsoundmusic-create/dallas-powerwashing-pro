Quick answers about washing in North Texas

The questions below are the ones that come up most. Each links to a page with more detail.

  • What does it cost? Angi lists an average of $293 for a house in Dallas. See the cost guide.
  • How often? About once a year for a house, per most guides. See the how often guide.
  • Can I pressure wash a shingle roof? The roofing trade says no. See roof soft washing.
  • Can the water go down the drain? Plain water may, within limits. Soapy water should not. See commercial washing.
  • Do you clean gutters? No. The line does not take gutter calls.

Understanding the words people use

Soft wash

Low pressure, usually with a cleaner doing the work. Fixr says typically under 500 PSI.

Power washing

Often means hot water, in cost guides such as HomeGuide. In daily speech it means the same as pressure washing.

Illicit discharge

City code language for anything other than stormwater going into a storm drain.

Certificate of insurance

A document from an insurer showing a company's coverage. Ask to see a current one.

A homeowner's last checks

Before work starts, take photos of any cracks, loose paint or worn caulk. Move what you do not want wet. Close windows. Tell the company about anything fragile. After it is done, walk the job together while the crew is still there.
