About the line

Who will I be connected with?

An independent, locally owned pressure washing company in the Dallas-Fort Worth area. It hears a short summary of your call first. The website does not name partner companies; the company introduces itself when it picks up.

Can I call about something other than pressure washing?

The same number takes AC and heating, roofing, water damage and mold calls for the Dallas area. The assistant asks what you need and routes the call.

Is there a contact form?

No. The site is phone only, so your request reaches a person who can act on it. See the contact page.

What happens to my details?

Your name, number, location and job are shared with the company handling your request so it can contact you and quote the work. The line does not sell personal information. The privacy policy has the details.

About methods

What is the difference between power washing and pressure washing?

Power washing uses heated water; pressure washing uses cold. Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution. The methods guide explains which surface needs which.

What is soft washing?

Cleaning with a solution applied at low pressure and given time to work, then rinsed gently. It is the usual method for siding, brick walls, stucco, roofs and wood. See house washing and soft washing.

Can my composite deck be pressure washed?

Depends on the brand and line. Trex allows gentle pressure washing on newer lines within its limits and not on early-generation boards. See deck cleaning.

About timing

How often should a house be washed?

Thumbtack's rule of thumb is at least once a year, more often with lots of rain, wind and storms. Shade and trees shorten the interval. See how often to wash a house in North Texas.

Can a house be washed in winter?

On mild days, yes. Washing right before a freeze leaves ice on steps and walks, so a careful company waits for a warmer stretch.

How long before I can paint or stain?

Sherwin-Williams suggests at least 24 to 48 hours after washing before painting. Wood decks and fences need to dry fully before stain; the stain maker's label says how long.

About water and rules

Can wash water go down the storm drain?

Not soapy or dirty water. The City of Dallas says letting soapy or polluted wash water reach the storm drain violates city code and the federal Clean Water Act for businesses, and Fort Worth requires mobile washers to collect wash water before it leaves the property. Ask any company how it handles runoff.

What about drought restrictions?

DFW cities use staged drought plans; Dallas's plan, for example, bans hosing and washing paved areas and buildings at Stage 3 unless a variance is granted, and limits that work to professional services using high efficiency equipment. Check your city's current stage in a dry summer; the town pages under service areas link to the local rules.
