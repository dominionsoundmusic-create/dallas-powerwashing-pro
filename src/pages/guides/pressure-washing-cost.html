Pressure washing cost in Dallas-Fort Worth comes down to three things: what is being cleaned, how big it is, and how dirty it is. National price guides give a useful starting range, and the rest of this page explains why your quote might land at the low or high end.

One note on where these numbers come from. Dallas Power Washing Pro is a free phone line that connects callers with independent local pressure washing companies. It does not set prices and has none of its own. Every figure below is from a published national guide, named and dated, and real DFW quotes can be higher or lower.

Pressure washing cost per square foot

General residential

Angi: about $0.10 to $0.50 per square foot. Housecall Pro's October 2026 benchmark: the same $0.10 to $0.50.

Driveways

HomeAdvisor: $0.30 to $0.55 per square foot. A standard 576 square foot driveway works out to about $175 to $320.

House walls

Fixr: $0.30 to $0.80 for pressure washing, $0.25 to $0.35 for soft washing. By material, brick and masonry sit at the low end and wood at the high end.

Per square foot pricing rewards size. Angi notes that larger jobs often get a lower rate per foot, which is one reason booking the house, driveway and walks together can cost less than calling three times.

What pushes a DFW quote up or down

  • Height and access. Two- and three-story homes need ladders or lifts. Angi's ranges jump from $150 to $750 for one story to $700 to $1,800 for three.
  • Material. Brick is cheaper per square foot to wash than wood, according to Angi, because wood takes more care.
  • How dirty it is. Heavy mildew on a shaded wall, oil on a driveway or rust fans all need pre-treatment and dwell time.
  • Method. Hot-water power washing costs more than cold-water pressure washing, Angi notes. Soft washing uses cleaning solution, which is a cost too.
  • Add-ons. Gutter faces, window rinse, sealing, stain or brightener.
  • Runoff control. Blocking drains and collecting water takes time, and it is the responsible way to work.
  • Sales tax. The Texas Comptroller treats pressure washing buildings and walks as a taxable cleaning service.

How to compare two quotes fairly

  1. Line by lineHouse, driveway, walks, fence and roof priced separately, so you can see what each costs.
  2. Method for each surfaceSoft wash for walls, roofs and wood; surface cleaner for flat concrete.
  3. What is includedGutter faces, eaves, steps, the curb strip, plant protection, runoff control.
  4. InsuranceProof of liability coverage, since Texas has no state license for this work.
  5. Tax and changesWhether sales tax is included, and that the price will not change once work starts.

A quote that is far below the others is usually leaving something out, or planning to use more pressure and less care to save time.

Getting a real price

Price guides tell you what is normal. They cannot see your north wall or the oil spot by the garage. When you call the free line, say what you want cleaned and roughly how big it is, and you are transferred to an independent DFW company that will look at the job and give you its own price. You are never charged by the line, and nothing about the call commits you.
