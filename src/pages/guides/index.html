The three questions almost everyone asks

What will it cost?

Angi's October 2026 guide puts most whole-house jobs nationally between $213 and $449. The cost guide lists every surface and what moves the price.

How often?

Thumbtack's rule of thumb is at least once a year, more with lots of rain, wind and storms. The frequency guide adjusts that for North Texas seasons.

Which method?

Power washing uses hot water, pressure washing cold, and soft washing low pressure with a cleaning solution. The methods guide matches each to a surface.
