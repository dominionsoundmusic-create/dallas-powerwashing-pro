Commercial pressure washing rates are usually quoted per square foot for open areas, per parking space for lots, by the hour for awkward jobs, or as a monthly contract for regular cleaning. The published ranges below give a business owner or property manager a way to check whether a quote is in the normal band.

Dallas Power Washing Pro is a free phone line that connects callers with independent local pressure washing companies in Dallas-Fort Worth. It has no prices of its own. The figures here come from published guides, named and dated. Real quotes in DFW vary with the site.

HomeAdvisor's guide lists commercial pressure washing at about $0.10 to $1 per square foot, compared with residential work that usually tops out around $0.50. Thumbtack quotes a Texas contractor saying that for commercial jobs, "typically pros will charge per square foot."

What moves a commercial quote

  • Grease and gum. Drive-thrus, restaurant pads and entries need degreaser and usually hot water.
  • Hot water. Angi notes power washing with heated water costs more than cold-water pressure washing.
  • Water recovery. Blocking drains and vacuuming up wash water takes equipment and time.
  • Hours. Overnight or before-opening work, cones and signage.
  • Frequency. A monthly or quarterly schedule is usually cheaper per visit than one-off calls.
  • Permits and tax. Fort Worth's mobile washer permit, and Texas sales tax on building and grounds cleaning.

Costs a quote should not leave out

Runoff handling

The City of Dallas says businesses violate city code and the federal Clean Water Act if water mixed with soap or other pollutants reaches the storm drain or street, and that when cleaning chemicals are used, the drain must be blocked and the contaminated water collected. A quote that does not mention how water is handled is either leaving it out or planning to ignore it.

The Fort Worth permit

Fort Worth requires a permit for mobile commercial cosmetic cleaning: $25 for the business permit and $25 to register each wash unit, valid for one year. If your property is in Fort Worth, ask the company for its permit.

Sales tax

The Texas Comptroller's publication on cleaning and janitorial services says pressure washing buildings, sidewalks and parking lots is taxable as building or grounds cleaning. Confirm whether the quote includes tax. For your own tax position, ask the Comptroller or your accountant.
