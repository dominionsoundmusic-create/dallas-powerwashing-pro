Pressure washing services in Dallas-Fort Worth fall into two families: surfaces that can take pressure, like concrete, and surfaces that cannot, like brick walls, shingles and wood. Every service below is matched to the right method, and every one starts with the same free call.

Dallas Power Washing Pro is a phone line and website. An automated assistant answers any hour, asks what you need cleaned, and transfers you to an independent, locally owned DFW pressure washing company that inspects, quotes and does the work. You pay that company directly.

How these differ

Residential pressure washing covers hard ground: patios, walks, pool decks, porches. House washing is the walls, done as a soft wash, because the Brick Industry Association warns high pressure can damage brick and mortar. Roof cleaning is soft wash only; the shingle makers' association says never to power wash a roof. Driveway cleaning is the one place a surface cleaner and real pressure belong, with oil and rust treated first.

Wood fences and decks are cleaned with a wood cleaner and a gentle rinse, then left to dry for several days before stain. Composite decks follow the maker's rules; Trex, for one, limits pressure washing on its newer boards and advises against it on early-generation boards.

Not offered through this line
