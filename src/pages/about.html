Why a phone line, and why these pages?

Finding someone to wash a house should not take an afternoon of calls.

Dallas Power Washing Pro gives people in Dallas-Fort Worth one free number. An automated assistant answers at any hour. If you need pressure or power washing, your call is transferred to an independent local company with a short spoken summary, and if no one answers, a message is taken.

The pages on this site exist for a second reason. Most washing advice ignores what matters here, such as clay soil, long pollen seasons and city rules on wash water. Pressure washing is not a state-licensed trade in Texas, so a homeowner has to know what to ask. This site tries to give you those questions, with sources you can check.

What this site will not do

  • Claim that any company is licensed, insured, certified or rated
  • Name the companies it connects you with
  • Publish prices as its own
  • Promise a response time
  • Post reviews or ratings

How money works here

The line is free to you. Here is the plain version.

Companies may pay a referral fee. You pay the company that does your work, directly, at the price you agree with it. Your call does not cost you anything beyond what your phone plan charges for any call. Your agreement, including any warranty, is with that company.

Where the line works

Dallas-Fort Worth only. See the service areas page for the cities, or read the services page for the six kinds of job. The Spanish button in the header translates each page with Google Translate.
