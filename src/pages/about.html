What this is

Dallas Power Washing Pro is a phone line and this website. It does not own a pressure washer, employ a crew or send anyone to your property. When you call, an automated assistant answers, any hour, asks what you need cleaned and roughly how big it is, and transfers your call to an independent, locally owned pressure washing company that works in Dallas-Fort Worth. That company inspects the job, prices it, does it and is responsible for it. You hire and pay it directly.

Dallas Power Washing Pro is a d/b/a of Dominion Digital Group, and its name matches its address, dallaspowerwashingpro.com. The line does not name an owner or staff on the site, because there is no crew to introduce: the people who do the work belong to the independent company you are connected with.

Why a phone line instead of another directory

Searching for pressure washing usually means a page of ads, review sites and contact forms that may or may not get answered. A phone line answered day and night, which puts you straight through to a company that does the work, is simpler. If the company is out on a job, your details are passed along for a call back instead of disappearing into an inbox.

The same number also takes AC and heating, roofing, water damage and mold calls for the Dallas area, which is why the assistant asks first what you need.

What the line will and will not do

It will

  • Answer any hour, every day
  • Ask what you need and connect you with an independent local company
  • Pass your details along for a call back if the company cannot pick up
  • Publish plain, sourced guides on cost, timing, method and Texas rules

It will not

  • Charge you anything
  • Clean, inspect, quote or schedule your job itself
  • Claim licenses, certifications or reviews it does not have
  • Promise a price, date or result for a company

How the website is written

Every page on this site is written for its own topic or town, with facts checked against published sources that are linked at the bottom of the page: city and state agencies, the National Weather Service, building material associations and named price guides. Where a fact could not be verified, it was left out. Prices on this site are always published ranges from those guides, never the line's own prices, because the line has none.
