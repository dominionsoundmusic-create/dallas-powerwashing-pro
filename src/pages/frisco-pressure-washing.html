First cleanings on newer homes

A newer house picks up dirt faster than people think. Light concrete shows tire marks and orange rust fans from sprinklers. Cream stone shows the yellow oak pollen that NBC 5's weather team describes coating homes and patios each spring. Fresh cedar or pine pickets gray within a few seasons.

The first cleaning is the one that sets the pattern. Done gently, with the right cleaner on stone and a soft wash on siding, it keeps a newer home looking newer. Done with too much pressure, it etches concrete and erodes fresh mortar joints.
