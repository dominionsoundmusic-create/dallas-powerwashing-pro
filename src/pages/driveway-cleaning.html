Why a Dallas driveway ages the way it does

Driveway cleaning in Dallas is rarely only about dirt. The soil under the slab is part of the story.

Much of this area sits on Blackland Prairie clay. The Texas Water Development Board describes the Blackland Prairie's best known soil, recommended as the Texas state soil and found from north of Dallas southward, as a clay that shrinks and swells markedly as moisture changes. A slab poured on soil like that gets lifted and dropped for years. So hairline cracks and slightly tilted panels are common, and they are worth noticing before anyone starts the machine.

Cleaning will not fix a tilted slab. It does let you see the true condition of the concrete, and it removes the algae that makes shaded panels slick.

Concrete cleaning and the street

A driveway slopes to the street for a reason. That also makes it the easiest way for dirty water to reach a storm drain.

Water only

The City of Dallas flyer says to block storm drains, divert the flow to grass and watch the driveway so water does not reach the street. Sandbags or weighted boards work.

With a cleaner

If cleaning chemicals are used, the flyer says to block the drains and collect all the contaminated water, for example with a wet vacuum. Ask how your company handles this.

Arlington gives one more rule worth following anywhere: never hose off a driveway or street to clean up a spill or leak.

Before the company arrives

  • Move vehicles, and leave them off the wet area until it dries.
  • Pick up loose gravel, pots and mats.
  • Point out cracks and raised joints.
  • Mention any new concrete, and ask how long it should cure first.
  • Say whether the walks, curb and patio are part of the job.

The price is usually tied to size. See the cost guide for per square foot figures. If the patio or front walk is included, tell the company at the start. Washing the house first means dirt runs down onto concrete that has not been done yet, so it makes sense to do the walls first and the driveway last.

Why a clean slab matters for safety

Green film on a shaded slab is slick when wet. If someone in your home is unsteady on their feet, a clean walk is worth more than a clean look.

Concrete cleaning for walks and patios

The same care applies to front walks and patios. Stamped or colored concrete has a surface layer that can be worn by close, narrow spray, so ask for a lower setting and a test spot.
