Fence cleaning in Dallas usually means a wood privacy fence that has gone from honey-colored to silver-gray, with dark mildew creeping up from the ground. Cleaning it properly brings the wood color back and gets it ready for stain. Cleaning it badly leaves the boards furry, gouged and streaked.

Gentle is the whole job

Fence pickets are thin, soft wood. A strong, narrow jet tears the surface fibers, leaving a fuzzy texture and lines you can see from across the yard. Family Handyman's guide to cleaning wood warns exactly this: high pressure can damage or scar wood, and stopping the wand in one place leaves start-and-stop marks.

A good fence cleaning leans on the cleaner, not the pressure. A wood cleaner is applied and given time to loosen the gray layer and kill mildew, then rinsed with a wide, low-pressure spray that follows the grain. Some companies follow with a brightener, often oxalic acid, which Family Handyman says lightens the wood and helps balance its pH before stain.

Why DFW fences weather so fast

Long, hot summers

The National Weather Service puts DFW's average first 100 degree day around July 1 and the last around August 27. That much sun breaks down bare wood quickly.

Shade and sprinklers

The shady side and the bottom boards stay damp from dew and irrigation, which is where black mildew starts.

Spring pollen

Oak pollen coats everything yellow in late winter and spring, according to NBC 5's weather team, and it sticks to rough, weathered boards.

Cleaning first, stain second

Most people clean a fence because they want to stain or seal it. The order and timing matter:

  1. CleanWood cleaner, gentle rinse, brightener if needed.
  2. Let it dry fullyAt least three dry days is a common rule of thumb; humid weather or rain resets the clock.
  3. Light sanding if the grain liftedSome fuzz is normal after cleaning; a careful job keeps it to a minimum.
  4. Stain or sealFollowing the product's own instructions for temperature and moisture.

Not every cleaning company stains, and staining is a separate quote. Ask when you call whether the company you are put through to does both or only the cleaning.
