How Much Does Pressure Washing Cost in Dallas? A Complete 2024 Pricing Guide

If you're a Dallas homeowner wondering whether to grab a garden hose or call in the pros, you're probably also asking yourself: how much does pressure washing actually cost? The short answer is that most Dallas homeowners spend between $150 and $500 for a professional pressure washing job — but the real answer depends on what you're cleaning, how big it is, and the condition it's in.

In this guide, we'll break down exactly what affects pressure washing prices in the Dallas area, give you realistic cost estimates for common jobs, and help you figure out when it makes sense to hire a professional versus renting a machine yourself. No fluff, just the numbers and details you need to make a smart decision.

  • Driveway pressure washing: $100 – $250 (single car) | $150 – $350 (two-car)
  • House exterior washing: $200 – $600 (depending on square footage)
  • Roof soft washing: $300 – $700
  • Patio or deck cleaning: $100 – $300
  • Fence washing: $100 – $250
  • Sidewalk and walkway cleaning: $75 – $200
  • Commercial parking lot or storefront: $200 – $1,000+

Most professional Dallas pressure washing companies also offer bundled packages. For example, combining a driveway, walkway, and patio cleaning in one visit typically saves you 15–25% compared to booking each service separately.

What Factors Affect Pressure Washing Prices in Dallas?

Understanding what drives the cost helps you avoid surprises on your invoice. Here are the biggest pricing factors local contractors consider:

1. Surface Size and Type

Most companies price by the square foot. Concrete driveways, brick, wood decks, and vinyl siding each require different pressure settings and cleaning solutions. Delicate surfaces like painted wood or older brick may require soft washing — a lower-pressure technique that uses specialized detergents — which can cost slightly more due to the chemistry involved.

2. Level of Dirt and Buildup

Dallas homeowners deal with a lot: red clay mud, tree pollen, algae from humid summers, and black mold that thrives in shaded areas. If your driveway hasn't been cleaned in five years or your roof has visible green and black streaking, expect to pay more. Heavily soiled surfaces take longer and sometimes require multiple passes or pre-treatment.

3. Accessibility

Second-story siding, steep rooflines, or areas blocked by landscaping take more time and equipment to reach. Jobs with limited access can add $50–$150 to the base price.

4. Travel and Location Within DFW

If you're located in a more distant suburb — say, Rockwall, Mansfield, or Waxahachie — some companies charge a small travel fee. Companies based in central Dallas or closer suburbs like Garland, Irving, or Mesquite typically won't add extra charges for most DFW addresses.

5. Time of Year

Spring and early summer are peak season for pressure washing in Dallas. Booking in fall or winter can sometimes get you a better rate, and the cooler weather is actually ideal for cleaning without rapid water evaporation.

DIY vs. Professional Pressure Washing in Dallas: Is It Worth It?

You can rent a gas-powered pressure washer at Home Depot or Lowe's in Dallas for about $75–$100 per day. So why do thousands of Dallas homeowners still hire professionals? Here's the honest comparison:

Factor DIY Professional
Cost $75–$120 (rental + supplies) $150–$600 depending on job
Time 4–8 hours for most homes 1–3 hours
Risk of Damage Higher (wrong PSI can damage siding, wood, mortar) Low (pros know correct pressure per surface)
Results Good for simple flat surfaces Excellent, especially for staining and mold
Insurance None — you're liable Fully insured and licensed

Our take: DIY is fine for a quick driveway rinse or a small patio. But for whole-house exteriors, roofs, stained concrete, or any surface you'd hate to accidentally damage, hiring a local Dallas professional is money well spent.
