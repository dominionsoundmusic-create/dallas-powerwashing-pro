How Much Does Pressure Washing Cost in Dallas? (2024 Pricing Guide)

If you've been looking at your driveway, fence, or home exterior and thinking it's time for a serious clean, you're not alone. Dallas homeowners deal with a unique combination of clay soil, summer heat, and unpredictable storms that leave surfaces looking dingy fast. But before you pick up the phone to book a service, you probably want to know: how much does pressure washing cost in Dallas?

The honest answer is — it depends. But don't worry. This guide breaks down everything you need to know about local pressure washing prices so you can budget smartly and avoid getting overcharged.

Average Pressure Washing Costs in Dallas, TX

In the Dallas–Fort Worth area, pressure washing services typically range from $150 to $600 for most residential jobs. Here's a quick snapshot of what you can expect to pay for the most common services:

  • Driveway pressure washing: $100 – $250
  • House exterior washing: $200 – $500
  • Deck or patio cleaning: $100 – $300
  • Fence washing: $100 – $250
  • Roof soft washing: $300 – $600
  • Sidewalk or walkway cleaning: $75 – $150
  • Concrete cleaning (pool deck, etc.): $150 – $400

Most Dallas pressure washing companies charge either a flat rate per project or by the square foot, typically between $0.10 and $0.50 per square foot depending on the surface type and level of grime.

What Factors Affect the Price of Pressure Washing in Dallas?

No two jobs are exactly the same, and several variables will influence your final quote. Here's what Dallas pressure washing professionals consider when pricing a job:

1. Size of the Area

The bigger the surface, the more time and water it takes. A 500-square-foot driveway costs less than a 2,000-square-foot one. Most companies use square footage as their baseline for pricing estimates.

2. Type of Surface

Concrete, brick, wood, and vinyl all require different pressure settings and sometimes different cleaning solutions. Delicate surfaces like wood decks or painted fences need softer pressure to avoid damage — which can add to the labor time and cost.

3. Level of Dirt and Buildup

Dallas homes near construction zones, heavily treed areas, or properties that haven't been washed in years often have significant mold, mildew, algae, or oil stains. Heavy buildup takes more passes, stronger detergents, and more time — all of which affect your price.

4. Accessibility

Two-story homes or surfaces that require equipment setup, ladders, or special positioning take longer and may carry a higher price. A simple one-story ranch home will almost always be cheaper to wash than a tall two-story colonial.

5. Additional Services

Some homeowners bundle services to save money — like having the driveway, sidewalk, and house exterior done in the same visit. Bundling often gets you a better per-surface rate than booking each separately.

Dallas-Specific Factors That Can Affect Your Cleaning Needs

Living in North Texas comes with some unique challenges that make regular pressure washing more than just a cosmetic concern:

  • Clay soil and red dirt: Dallas's famous heavy clay soil tracks onto driveways and walkways constantly, creating stubborn staining that standard washing won't fully address without proper technique.
  • Heat and UV exposure: Intense Texas summers cause algae and mildew to thrive on shaded surfaces like north-facing walls and patios.
  • Storm runoff: Spring storms can leave debris, mud, and organic staining across your entire exterior practically overnight.
  • Tree coverage: Many Dallas neighborhoods have mature oak and pecan trees that drop tannins, sap, and pollen onto surfaces year-round.

These factors mean Dallas homeowners typically benefit from pressure washing at least once a year — and sometimes twice, once in spring and once heading into fall.

Pressure Washing vs. Soft Washing: Which Do You Need?

This is a question we hear all the time from Dallas homeowners, and the answer can affect both the cost and the outcome of your service.

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water to blast away dirt, grime, and stains. It's ideal for hard surfaces like concrete driveways, brick walkways, and stone patios.

Soft washing uses low pressure combined with specialized cleaning solutions to safely clean more delicate surfaces like roof shingles, painted wood siding, stucco, and vinyl. It's more effective at killing biological growth like mold and algae at the root level, not just washing it off temporarily.

Soft washing typically costs a bit more due to the cleaning agents involved, but it's the right call for roofs and siding. Using high-pressure water on these surfaces can cause serious damage, void warranties, and even allow moisture intrusion into your home.

A reputable Dallas pressure washing company will recommend the right method for each surface — not just default to one approach for everything.

How to Get the Best Value on Pressure Washing in Dallas

You don't need to overpay to get excellent results. Here are a few tips to stretch your budget further:

  • Bundle multiple surfaces. Get the driveway, walkway, and house done together for better overall pricing.
  • Schedule during off-peak times. Late fall and winter tend to be slower seasons, which can mean more competitive rates.
  • Get at least two or three quotes. Pricing varies between companies, so comparing quotes ensures you're getting a fair deal.
  • Ask about recurring service discounts. Many Dallas companies offer reduced rates for customers who schedule regular cleanings throughout the year.
  • Don't choose on price alone. The cheapest option isn't always the best. Look for licensed, insured companies with solid reviews from Dallas-area homeowners.

Is Professional Pressure Washing Worth It in Dallas?

Short answer: absolutely. Beyond just improving your home's curb appeal, professional pressure washing in Dallas offers real, practical benefits:

  • Removes harmful mold and mildew before it causes structural damage
  • Extends the life of your driveway, deck, fence, and siding
  • Prepares surfaces properly for painting or staining
  • Can increase your home's resale value — first impressions matter
  • Eliminates slip hazards caused by algae and moss on walkways

Hiring a professional also means the job gets done safely, with the right equipment and the right technique for each surface — something DIY rentals can't always guarantee.
