How Much Does Pressure Washing Cost in Dallas? (2024 Pricing Guide)

If you've been looking at your driveway, fence, or home exterior and thinking it's overdue for a deep clean, you're not alone. Dallas homeowners deal with a unique combination of red clay soil, summer humidity, and heavy pollen that leaves surfaces looking grimy fast. But before you hire someone or rent a machine, you probably want to know: how much does pressure washing cost in Dallas?

The honest answer is — it depends. Service prices vary based on what you're cleaning, the size of the area, and the condition of the surface. In this guide, we'll break down typical pressure washing costs in the Dallas-Fort Worth area so you can budget smart and know exactly what to expect.

Average Pressure Washing Costs in Dallas, TX

Across the Dallas metro area, most professional pressure washing services charge either by the square foot or by the job type. Here's a general overview of what homeowners typically pay:

  • Driveway pressure washing: $100 – $250
  • House exterior washing: $200 – $600+
  • Deck or patio cleaning: $100 – $300
  • Fence washing: $75 – $200
  • Roof soft washing: $300 – $600
  • Sidewalk or walkway cleaning: $50 – $150
  • Pool deck cleaning: $150 – $400

Per square foot, most Dallas pressure washing companies charge between $0.10 and $0.50 per square foot, depending on the surface type and level of buildup. Concrete driveways tend to be on the lower end, while wood decks or delicate surfaces requiring soft washing run higher.

What Factors Affect the Price of Pressure Washing?

Not every job is created equal. Several factors can push your quote higher or lower than the averages above. Here's what Dallas pressure washing companies look at when pricing a job:

1. Size of the Area

This is the biggest factor. A 500-square-foot driveway costs significantly less than a 2,000-square-foot one. When getting quotes, always confirm whether pricing is per square foot or flat-rate per job.

2. Type of Surface

Concrete can handle high-pressure blasting, but wood, stucco, and vinyl siding require a gentler soft washing technique that uses lower pressure and specialized cleaning solutions. Soft washing typically costs a bit more due to the added care and materials involved.

3. Level of Dirt and Staining

In Dallas, we deal with oil stains from cars, rust from sprinkler systems, algae from our humid summers, and that stubborn red clay mud from North Texas soil. Heavy staining or long-neglected surfaces take more time and effort — which means higher costs.

4. Accessibility

Two-story homes, steep roofs, or surfaces blocked by landscaping require additional equipment and labor. Expect to pay more if your property has hard-to-reach areas.

5. Add-On Services

Many Dallas homeowners bundle multiple services for a better overall price. Cleaning your driveway, walkway, and patio together is almost always cheaper per square foot than booking each service separately.

Pressure Washing vs. Soft Washing: Which Does Your Home Need?

This is a question we get a lot, and it's an important one for both results and your wallet.

Pressure washing uses high-pressure water to blast away dirt, grime, and debris. It's ideal for hard surfaces like concrete driveways, brick walls, and stone patios.

Soft washing uses low-pressure water combined with eco-friendly cleaning solutions to safely remove mold, mildew, algae, and organic staining. It's the right choice for roof cleaning, vinyl siding, wood fences, and painted surfaces.

Using the wrong technique can cause real damage — high pressure on a wood deck can splinter the surface, and high pressure on roof shingles can strip the protective granules right off. A reputable Dallas pressure washing company will assess your surfaces and recommend the right approach before starting any work.

Is Professional Pressure Washing Worth the Cost in Dallas?

Absolutely — and here's why Dallas homeowners should think of it as an investment, not just an expense:

  • Curb appeal: A clean driveway and fresh exterior can add thousands to your home's perceived value. If you're thinking about selling, pressure washing is one of the highest-ROI upgrades you can make before listing.
  • Preventative maintenance: Mold, mildew, and algae aren't just ugly — they can cause real damage over time. Regular cleaning protects your siding, deck, and roof from deterioration.
  • Health and safety: Algae and mold on walkways and driveways create slippery surfaces. Pressure washing removes those hazards before someone gets hurt.
  • Dallas climate demands it: The combination of heat, humidity, and those famous Texas dust storms means surfaces here get dirtier faster than in many other parts of the country. Once or twice a year is a reasonable cleaning schedule for most homes.

Renting a pressure washer yourself might seem like a cost-saving option, but factor in the rental fee ($50–$100/day), your time, cleaning solutions, and the very real risk of damaging your property without proper technique — and hiring a professional starts to look like the smarter choice.

How to Get the Best Price on Pressure Washing in Dallas

Want to make sure you're getting a fair deal? Here are a few tips from our experience serving Dallas homeowners:

  1. Get at least two or three quotes. Prices can vary widely between companies. A quick comparison ensures you're not overpaying.
  2. Ask what's included. Does the quote cover cleaning solutions? Will they pre-treat stubborn stains? Knowing what's in the price prevents surprises.
  3. Bundle your services. Combining a driveway, patio, and house wash in one visit saves on labor and usually comes with a discount.
  4. Check reviews and licensing. The cheapest quote isn't always the best value. Look for a company with strong local reviews and proper insurance in case of accidental damage.
  5. Ask about seasonal deals. Spring and fall are the busiest seasons for Dallas pressure washing. Booking in the off-season can sometimes get you a better rate.
